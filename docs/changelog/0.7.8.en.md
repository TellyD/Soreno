# Soreno 0.7.8

## Changes

- On the very first launch, Soreno opens on **Projects**; after that, it reopens the workspace you were in.
- In a project’s form, the Linear project picker shows the team or teams of each Linear project.
- The Kanban project selector is wider.
- In **Settings** > **General**, the **Terminal** section now comes after **Encrypted notes**, just before **Diagnostics**.

## Fixes

- The Kanban shows a renamed project’s current name, in issues and in filters, with its environment (local, WSL, SSH).
