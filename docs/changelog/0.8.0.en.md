# Soreno 0.8.0

## What’s new

### Project group services

In a project group, the **Services** button and its panel gather the services of every SSH project in the group, sorted by project, each with its own **Start all**. They are available from any project of the group, one of its worktrees or the overview, and the panel stays open or closed for the whole group.

## Changes

- A group’s row in the project list is simpler: **Open overview** sits right on the group’s row, and expanding a group always shows its overview sessions, even after **Collapse all projects**.
