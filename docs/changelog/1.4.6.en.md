# Soreno 1.4.6

## What’s new

- The project rail has a **View options** menu:
  - **Show** picks the details visible on each row: **Host**, **Branch**, **Project** and **Group summary**;
  - **Grouping** sorts sessions by **Project** or by **Status**, like the **Priority view**, and the choice is remembered;
  - **Only projects with agents** and **Only active sessions** filter the list.
- Grouped by status, sessions that are neither to handle nor in progress show in an **Inactive** section instead of being hidden.

## Changes

- In the **Add a project** menu, **New session** sorts projects by rail section, in rail order.
