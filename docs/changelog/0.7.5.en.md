# Soreno 0.7.5

## What’s new

### Encrypted notes

A note can be encrypted with a passphrase: **Encrypt**, in the note’s title bar, encrypts it along with its images. The first time, Soreno asks you to create the passphrase (at least 8 characters), which then protects every encrypted note, in every vault. Keep it somewhere safe: without it, those notes are permanently unreadable.

- An encrypted note shows locked until you enter the passphrase. **Lock** closes every encrypted note, and they lock by themselves after the idle delay chosen in **Settings** > **General** > **Encrypted notes** > **Automatic lock** (10 minutes by default).
- **Remove encryption** saves the note and its images in plain text again, after confirmation.
- **Settings** > **General** > **Encrypted notes** > **Passphrase** lets you change it; notes stay readable.

### Tasks tab

The activity bar gains a **Tasks** tab. **Kanban**, **Notes** and **Tasks** can be reordered there by drag and drop, and the order is kept after a restart.

## Changes

- The **Diff options** menu no longer offers **File details** (line endings, final newline, size).
- Commenting on a mockup element asks OMP for a targeted edit rather than a full rewrite, with a full rewrite as a fallback if the edit doesn’t apply; the commented version stays shown until the edit is applied.
- Messages in the Git **History** list are centered.

## Fixes

- Switching vaults from **Tasks** shows the new vault’s tasks instead of going back to notes.
- Over SSH, reopening a session whose former client got stuck on a lost connection works again, instead of hanging.
